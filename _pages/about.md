Welcome to my page. This is Zihan Lou. I'm a first-year Ph.D. student majoring in statistics from [Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science](https://stat.uiowa.edu) at [the University of Iowa](https://uiowa.edu).

My research interests lie in the field of machine learning and functional data. I am doing researches about online functional data analysis optimization with SGD(stochastic gradient descent) algorithms under supervision of [Prof. Hua liu](https://liuhuasufe.github.io/). Before that, I did some research about timeseries analysis. I applied timeseries analysis to predicting China's economy development and unemployment under supervison of [Prof. Hua Liu](https://liuhuasufe.github.io/) and [Prof. Cong Li](http://sef.xjtu.edu.cn/info/1086/9392.htm).
